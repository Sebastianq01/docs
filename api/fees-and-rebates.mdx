updown charges a fee only on the **taker** side of a fill. If your order was **resting** on the book when another trader's order filled against it, you are the **maker**: you pay nothing, and you earn a **maker rebate**, a share of the fee the taker paid.

The rebate is automatic. There is nothing to sign up for or turn on. It applies to every resting order you place, through the API or on the website.

<Note>
The program's full terms are the [API and Liquidity Provider Agreement](https://www.updown.fast/liquidity-providers). By using the API or resting an order on the book, you agree to it.
</Note>

## Maker and taker

| | You are the… | Fee | Rebate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your order fills against an order already resting on the book | **Taker** | Taker fee | — |
| Another order fills against your resting order | **Maker** | `0` | A share of that taker's fee |

An order that crosses the book when you place it is a taker for the part that fills at once. Whatever is left rests, and is a maker from then on. Set [`post_only: true`](/api/orders#place-an-order) to make sure an order only ever rests: if any of it would trade at once, it is refused with `POST_ONLY_CROSS`.

## The taker fee

<Note>
The volume-tiered taker fee takes effect on **November 22, 2026 at 8:30 PM ET** (November 23 at 01:30 UTC).
</Note>

Your taker fee depends on your volume tier and season discount:

```text
fee = contracts × effective_rate × p × (1 − p)
```

Here, `p` is the fill price in dollars. Your base rate uses your trailing taker notional over the **30 completed US Eastern calendar days**, excluding today. Taker notional is contracts × price paid. Only live taker fills count toward this volume.

| Tier | Trailing taker notional | Base rate | Basis points |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Under $10,000 | `0.095` | 950 |
| 2 | $10,000 to under $50,000 | `0.085` | 850 |
| 3 | $50,000 to under $250,000 | `0.075` | 750 |
| 4 | $250,000 or more | `0.0695` | 695 |

Tiers recompute daily after midnight ET, including across daylight-saving changes. Today's fills enter the calculation after today closes in ET. An order keeps the rate it was submitted with, even if your rate changes before it fills.

The maximum undiscounted fee is **2.375¢ per contract**, at 50¢ in Tier 1. The fee falls toward zero as the price approaches $0 or $1. It is charged in dollars and rounded to the millionth of a dollar. A taker order's actual fees show in `fees_paid`. Makers pay zero, and nothing is charged at settlement.

### New accounts

Day 1 is the ET calendar date of your first live fill, whether you were the maker or taker. These are calendar days, not rolling 24-hour periods.

- **Days 1–7:** your base rate is `0.0695` (Tier 4).
- **Days 8–30:** use the tier for `max(trailing30, first7 × 30/7)`, where `first7` is your taker notional over your first seven ET calendar days and `trailing30` is your taker notional over the 30 completed ET calendar days.
- **Day 31 onward:** use your trailing 30 completed ET days of taker notional alone.

### Season discounts

Your season discount applies on top of your base rate, up to **25% off**. The effective coefficient is floored to whole basis points after the discount. For example, a 25% discount on 695 bps gives `floor(695 × 0.75) = 521` bps, an effective rate of `0.0521`.

### Read your effective rate

Call authenticated [`GET /trade-api/v1/portfolio/fee_tier`](/api-reference/get-fee-tier) for your `tier`, effective `taker_fee_rate` after the season discount, `trailing_30d_taker_volume`, `next_tier_threshold`, and `onboarding_ends_at`. On the website, use the fee-tier card on your **Portfolio** page.

Each series also publishes the current schedule in [`fee_schedule`](/api-reference/list-series). Read it and your account's effective rate from the API rather than hard-coding a coefficient. A null `tier` means the flat fallback is in use, rather than the volume-tiered schedule.

## Rebate rates

Your share of the taker fee depends on the market's category (the series' `category`):

| Category | Your share of the taker fee |
|---|---|
| `crypto` | 30% |
| `stocks` | 40% |
| `preipo` | 40% |
| `commodities` | 40% |

Markets in other categories earn no rebate for now. Each fill earns the rate in force when it filled. We give at least 7 days' notice before a rate goes down.

Rebates are a share of the taker fee **actually paid**, after the taker's volume tier and season discount.

**Example.** A taker past onboarding with under $10,000 of trailing taker notional and no season discount buys 100 contracts from your resting order at 50¢ on a crypto market. The taker pays 100 × 0.095 × 0.50 × 0.50 = **$2.375** in fees. You pay nothing and earn 30% of it: **$0.7125**.

## Which fills earn

A fill earns a rebate when the taker is another trader in good standing. It earns nothing when the other side of the fill is your own account, or an account linked to yours (a [team](/platform/team-access) member of your account, or an account you are a member of). [Self-trade prevention](/api/errors) stops your order from filling against your own resting order. Don't rely on it as your only control.

The agreement lists who can earn and the conduct rules. Wash trading, spoofing, or splitting activity across accounts to earn more voids rebates.

## Getting paid

Rebates are **paid automatically**. There is nothing to claim.

- About once a minute, everything you have earned and not yet been paid is sent to your updown wallet as cash.
- Payouts go out in whole cents, so an amount under 1¢ waits for the next payout.
- Payouts need the current Terms of Service accepted.
- Your **Portfolio** page shows your total rebates beside **Joined**. Open it for a log by day: what you earned, and each payout with its transaction.

There is no API endpoint for rebates yet. Your [API keys page](https://www.updown.fast/api-keys) also shows what you have earned each day.

## Program start

The program has run since **October 17, 2026** (midnight US Eastern). Every eligible maker fill from then on earns.
