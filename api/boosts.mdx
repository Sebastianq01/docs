---
title: "Boost orders"
description: "Place, track and sell Boost and multiplier orders with a trade key: one order that pays more the further the price closes your way."
---

A Boost order is one order on the window that's open now. It pays more the further the price closes your way. It's the same order the website places: the same server prices it, and the same limits and settlement apply. Placing and selling need an API key with the **Trade** permission; reading needs **Read**. See [Authentication](/api/authentication).

<Warning>
Boost orders are **real money**. The stake is paid from your account's cash the moment the order is placed. Orders placed through the API never use trade credit.
</Warning>

## Two kinds

| `kind` | What you get |
|---|---|
| `boost` (default) | The regular Up / Down contract plus one **ride** from the price at the moment you buy. Part of the stake buys the regular contract, enough that a right call pays your whole stake back. The rest pays a fixed amount for every $1 the asset closes past the price when you bought, up to a cap of **100× your stake**. If the regular contract alone pays more than that, its payout is the cap. |
| `multiplier` | The website's Multiplier at the setting you send in `multiplier`, `"1"` to `"10"`. How it pays is on the [Multiplier](/interval/multiplier#how-it-pays) page. |

For example, a $10 Boost on Up when Up costs 65¢: $6.50 buys 10 contracts, so any close at or above the price to beat pays at least $10. The other $3.50 is the ride. It adds to the payout for every $1 the asset closes above the price at the moment you bought.

On a `boost` order, `multiplier` in the response is the cap divided by the stake (`max_payout` ÷ `stake`), not a setting you chose.

## Where and when

- **Which markets.** Boost is offered on the markets where the Multiplier is offered to your account. Anywhere else, an order answers `403 BOOST_UNAVAILABLE`.
- **The current window only.** `market_ticker` must name the window that's open now; find it with [`GET /markets?status=open`](/api-reference/list-markets). Any other window answers `400 MARKET_NOT_OPEN`.
- **Not in the last minute**, and not while the Multiplier is [paused](/interval/multiplier) (around the stock market's open and close, US data releases, and so on). Both answer `400 MARKET_NOT_OPEN`.
- **Live keys only.** There is no practice Boost: a test key's order answers `403 BOOST_UNAVAILABLE`, and its list is always empty.
- **Check `mode`.** `live` is real money. `shadow` means the market isn't open to real money for your account yet: the order is recorded, but no money moved.

## Place a Boost

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.updown.fast/trade-api/v1/portfolio/boosts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $UPDOWN_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "market_ticker": "btc-15m-261102-1600-1615",
    "outcome": "up",
    "stake": "10",
    "kind": "boost",
    "expected_price": "0.65",
    "client_order_id": "boost-000123"
  }'
```

```json 201 Created
{
  "boost": {
    "boost_id": "6f1c2a8e-3b4d-4e5f-9a1b-2c3d4e5f6a7b",
    "client_order_id": "boost-000123",
    "market_ticker": "btc-15m-261102-1600-1615",
    "outcome": "up",
    "kind": "boost",
    "mode": "live",
    "status": "open",
    "stake": "10.00",
    "multiplier": "100.00",
    "max_payout": "1000.00",
    "price_to_beat": "110350.25",
    "value": "9.96",
    "sell_proceeds": "9.41",
    "created_ts_ms": 1793635262114,
    "close_ts_ms": 1793636100000
  }
}
```

| Field | |
|---|---|
| `market_ticker` | The window that's open now. |
| `outcome` | `up` or `down`. |
| `stake` | Dollars as a string, up to two decimals. **$1 to $100**, and no more than your key's max order. |
| `kind` | `boost` (default) or `multiplier`. |
| `multiplier` | `kind: "multiplier"` only, and required there: `"1"` to `"10"`, up to two decimals. |
| `expected_max_payout` | Optional. The order is refused (`400 ORDER_REJECTED`) if its max payout comes out more than 10% below this, because the price moved. |
| `expected_price` | Optional, `kind: "boost"` only. The contract price you priced on. The order is refused (`400 ORDER_REJECTED`) if the part of your stake that rides comes out more than 10% smaller than at this price. |
| `client_order_id` | **Required.** Your id for the order; see below. |

The response is the order after it's placed, usually `open`. It reads `pending` while the stake is still being taken.

## Retrying safely with `client_order_id`

The rules are the same as for [orders](/api/orders#retrying-safely-with-client_order_id):

- **Same id, same order** → you get back the order already placed. Never a second one.
- **Same id, different order** → `409 IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`.

When a request times out or answers `503 EXCHANGE_UNAVAILABLE`, resend it with the **same** `client_order_id`, or look it up with [`GET /portfolio/boosts?client_order_id=…`](/api-reference/list-boosts). Never retry with a new id: if the first request went through, a new id places a second order.

A `failed` order keeps its id. Nothing moved, so to try again, send a new `client_order_id`.

Ids are 1 to 64 characters (letters, digits, `.` `_` `:` `-`) and unique per account. Boost orders and book orders keep separate ids, so the same id can be used once on each.

## Order status

| `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `pending` | The stake is being taken. |
| `open` | Live until the window closes. It can be sold early. |
| `selling` | Your sale is being paid. |
| `sold` | Sold back early. `payout` is what the sale paid. |
| `settling` | The window closed and the payout is being sent. |
| `settled` | Paid out, or lost. `payout` is what it paid (`0.00` on a loss). |
| `void` | Refunded: the window's result couldn't be read. |
| `failed` | The stake was never taken. Nothing happened. |

## Track your orders

| Endpoint | Returns |
|---|---|
| [`GET /portfolio/boosts`](/api-reference/list-boosts) | Your Boost and multiplier orders, newest first, including ones placed on the website or in the app (those have no `client_order_id`). Filter by `market_ticker`, `client_order_id` or `status`; `limit` 1 to 100, default 50. |
| [`GET /portfolio/boosts/{boost_id}`](/api-reference/get-boost) | One order. |

While an order is open, `value` is what it's worth at the current price and `sell_proceeds` is what selling it right now pays. Each is left out when it can't be worked out. After the close, `close_price` is the settlement price (or the price it was sold at) and `payout` is what it paid.

There's no websocket channel for Boost orders yet, so poll these endpoints. Boost orders aren't part of [`GET /portfolio/orders`](/api-reference/list-orders), [`/positions`](/api-reference/get-positions) or [`/fills`](/api-reference/get-fills), which cover the order book.

`credit_used` and `paid` show only on orders placed on the website or in the app with trade credit. There, `paid` is what reached the wallet: the payout less the credit.

## Sell early

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.updown.fast/trade-api/v1/portfolio/boosts/6f1c2a8e-3b4d-4e5f-9a1b-2c3d4e5f6a7b/sell \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $UPDOWN_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "expected_proceeds": "9.41" }'
```

The order is sold back for its current `sell_proceeds`, paid to your cash, and the response shows it `sold`.

- `sell_proceeds` is **below** `value`, so buying and selling straight back costs money.
- `expected_proceeds` is optional. With it, the sale is refused (`400 ORDER_REJECTED`) if it would pay more than 10% less.
- Only an `open` order can be sold, and not in the window's last minute.
- A sale has no `client_order_id`. If it answers `503 EXCHANGE_UNAVAILABLE`, get the order before trying again: `selling` or `sold` means it went through.

## At the close

Open orders settle automatically on the window's result: `settling`, then `settled`, with the payout sent to your cash. If the result still can't be read 6 hours after the close, the stake is refunded and the order reads `void`.

## Limits

The tightest of these wins:

1. **Your key's limits.** The key's max order applies to the stake: trade keys start at **$20 per order**, so raise the key's max order to stake more. The key's daily limit counts its Boost stakes of the last 24 hours alongside its book fills and resting buys; [`GET /account`](/api-reference/get-account) shows them together as `daily_used_usd`. A key limited to some series can only place Boost orders on those.
2. **The stake**, the same as on the website: **$1 to $100** per order. There's no limit on how many orders you place on a window or in a day, beyond the cash they need and your key's limits.
3. **The order budget.** Each new Boost order costs one unit of your key's order budget (10 a second, bursts of 20), like a book order. Sales don't. See [Rate limits](/api/rate-limits).

Going over a limit answers `400 LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, with a message that says which.

Before your first order, the account must have accepted the current Terms of Service and **allowed trading** on its wallet (a one-time step in the app). Without them, an order answers `403 FORBIDDEN` and says which step is missing.

## Errors

| HTTP | `code` | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 400 | `BAD_REQUEST` | A field is missing or invalid; `message` says which. |
| 400 | `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` | Not enough cash for the stake. |
| 400 | `LIMIT_EXCEEDED` | Over a key limit, or a stake outside $1 to $100; `message` says which. |
| 400 | `MARKET_NOT_OPEN` | Not the window that's open now, its last minute, or a Multiplier pause. |
| 400 | `ORDER_REJECTED` | Refused for another reason, such as the price moved past your `expected_*` field or there's no fresh price; `message` says why. Nothing was placed or sold. |
| 403 | `BOOST_UNAVAILABLE` | Boost isn't offered to this account on this market, or the key is a test key. |
| 403 | `FORBIDDEN` | The key lacks the permission, or the account can't trade yet (Terms, allow trading). |
| 404 | `ORDER_NOT_FOUND` | No Boost order with that id on your account. |
| 409 | `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` | That `client_order_id` was used for a different order. |
| 409 | `ACCOUNT_BUSY` | Another request on your account is still running. Retry in a moment with the same `client_order_id`. |
| 429 | `RATE_LIMITED` | Too many new orders. Wait `Retry-After` seconds. |
| 503 | `TRADING_PAUSED` | Trading is paused; `message` says why. |
| 503 | `EXCHANGE_UNAVAILABLE` | We couldn't confirm the stake or the sale. Look the order up before trying again. |
